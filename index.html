ABOUT RIMBIT CONSTRUCTION

Materials That Keep Construction Moving

Construction projects do not stop because materials are missing. Rimbit helps contractors, developers, builders and project teams source the materials they need, when they need them.

Whether it is a planned bulk order for a multi-storey development or specific materials needed on demand to keep daily site work flowing, Rimbit focuses on making material sourcing simpler, more dependable, and completely transparent.

  • Practical supply: Sourcing directly from verified cement plants, steel fabricators, and quarries.
  • Contractor focused: We understand project schedules, offloading challenges, and material quality standards.
  • Straightforward service: Honest lead times, clear invoicing, and direct communication without unnecessary delays.

Planned & On Demand

Order scheduled delivery or request rapid sourcing for items needed on site urgently.

Kenya-Wide Coverage
