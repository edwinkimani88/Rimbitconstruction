WHAT WE DO

Straightforward Material Supply Solutions

Rimbit connects your construction site with reliable quarries, certified cement plants, and steel mills — delivering the materials you need with dependable coordination.

SERVICE 01

Construction Material Supply

Reliable sourcing and supply of quality materials for ongoing and new construction projects. We maintain strong commercial partnerships with Kenya's foremost cement producers (Bamburi, Simba, Mombasa Cement), licensed Ndarugu quarries for machine cut stones, river sand suppliers in Machakos/Makueni, and primary steel rolling mills.

  • Certified standard and high-strength Portland cement (32.5R & 42.5N)
  • Clean coarse river sand, fine plastering sand, and machine-crushed ballast
  • BS 4449 Grade 500 TMT deformed steel rebars & BRC structural mesh
  • Standard 6" and 9" machine-cut foundation and walling stones

SERVICE 02

On-Demand Sourcing

Construction schedules change, unexpected shortages happen, and architectural designs frequently require specialized grades that are not kept in everyday neighborhood hardware stock. Rimbit's on-demand sourcing network steps in to locate, verify, and transport specific materials according to project requirements.

  • Heavy D16, D20, and D25 reinforcement bars for load-bearing structures
  • Custom box profile roofing sheet lengths cut to site schedule
  • Specialized heavy-duty Cabro paving blocks and road curbs
  • Formwork film-faced shuttering plywood and scaffolding props

SERVICE 03

Project Material Supply

Continuous, scheduled supply support for contractors, real estate developers, and construction project teams requiring ongoing supply partnerships. Rather than managing dozens of fragmented individual transporters and suppliers, Rimbit provides a single, organized point of supply accountability.

  • Staged deliveries synchronized with your structural pour dates and casting schedules
  • Consistent batch quality and verified KEBS compliance certificates
  • Consolidated statements and clear itemized billing
Discuss Project Supply
SERVICE 04

Delivery Coordination

Getting materials delivered to a Kenyan construction site is half the battle. From navigating access restrictions and axle load rules to arranging appropriate tippers, flatbeds, or crane offloading, Rimbit handles the transportation logistics so your site team can focus on building.

  • Tipper trucks for sand, ballast, and quarry stone (10T, 14T, 20T, 28T)
  • Flatbed vehicles for straight bundles of 12-metre steel bars
  • Enclosed moisture-protected haulage for cement bags
  • Site offloading assistance and route coordination
