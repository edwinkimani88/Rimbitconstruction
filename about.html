OUR POSITIONING

Materials That Keep Construction Moving

Construction projects do not stop because materials are missing. Rimbit helps contractors, developers, builders and project teams source the materials they need, when they need them.

Rimbit Construction Limited is a Kenyan construction-materials supplier. We make material sourcing simpler, dependable, and transparent — whether you require scheduled bulk deliveries for ongoing multi-storey developments or on-demand materials needed urgently to avoid site downtime.

HOW WE OPERATE

Practical Supply. Straightforward Service.

We believe material sourcing should be transparent, honest, and grounded in real site conditions.

Genuine Quality Materials

We work directly with certified Kenyan manufacturers and verified quarries, ensuring genuine cement grades, certified BS standard steel rebars, clean river sand, and properly cut building stones.

Honest Supply Timelines

No misleading promises. We communicate realistic delivery schedules and offloading logistics so your project team can plan concrete pouring, masonry, and steel-fixing accurately.

Site-Direct Coordination

Whether your building site is in Ruiru, Kilimani, Syokimau, Karen, Thika, or outside Nairobi, we manage the vehicular haulage and offloading coordination directly to your site gate.
